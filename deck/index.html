Exact Copilot Prompts — Use These Live

STEP 1 — SUMMARISE
"Summarise this email thread in exactly 5 bullet points. Include: the core problem, who is involved, what has been raised, what is unclear, and what needs a decision."
STEP 2 — DECISIONS
"From this thread, list only the decisions that have been formally agreed. Do not include opinions or suggestions."
STEP 3 — ACTION ITEMS ⚑ KEY
"Extract all action items. For each: state the action, the owner, and the due date. If the owner is not explicitly named in the thread, write [CONFIRM OWNER]. If the date is not stated, write [CONFIRM DATE]. DO NOT INVENT."
STEP 4 — OPEN QUESTIONS
"List all unresolved questions or issues from this thread that need a clear answer before the team can proceed."
STEP 5 — DRAFT REPLY ⚑ VERIFY BEFORE SENDING
"Draft a professional reply to the Product Manager acknowledging the open items, confirming what I will do, and flagging what needs to be decided before Friday. Tone: collaborative, senior. I will review before sending."

        throw new Exception("Failed");
