Facilitator Guide

VEKA GenAI Excellence

The trainer is not there to teach AI. The trainer is there to keep the room moving, challenge weak outputs, and tie every exercise back to the VEKA factory floor.

Secret Trainer Mode

Press Shift + T in the interactive deck to reveal the trainer notes panel at the bottom of the screen. This panel is invisible to participants.

The 3 Trainer Rules

  • Never touch their keyboard. Let them struggle for 30 seconds. If they are stuck, ask them: "What do you want the AI to do? Tell me in plain English." Then say, "Type exactly what you just told me."
  • Challenge the first draft. If someone looks proud of a generic email, ask them: "Where is the PO number? How does the supplier know this is VEKA?" Force them to add Context.
  • The points are the excuse. Use the leaderboard aggressively. Award +3 for the best prompt, +1 for helping a neighbour. It gamifies the struggle.

Hour-by-Hour Guide

Hour 1: Ignite
60 min

Goal: Every single person in the room gets a usable output they could send today. Overcome the "blank page" fear.

Watch For
People staring blankly. Go to them immediately. They don't know what task to pick.
Debrief Question
"Which word in the 'After' demo could only have come from knowing the situation?"
Rescue Line (Stuck participant)
"Don't invent a scenario. Just tell me one email you wrote last week that took too long. Let's make the AI write it."
Hour 2: The Anatomy of a Prompt
60 min

Goal: Teach C·T·P·F (Context, Tone, Persona, Format) so it becomes their permanent mental model for briefing AI.

Watch For
People skipping Tone and Format. They usually only give Context.
Debrief Question
"Which of the four levers changed the output the most for you?"
Rescue Line (Weak output)
"Your output is too long. Which lever fixes that? (Format). Tell it: 'Under 100 words'."
Hour 3: High-Stakes Writing
60 min

Goal: Produce a vendor negotiation, a shift handover, and a crisis note. Prove that AI can handle nuance and risk.

Watch For
Outputs that apologise too much in the crisis note.
Debrief Question
"Would you rather receive a 'sorry-first' email or a 'fact-first' email at 5pm on a Friday?"
Rescue Line (Apologetic tone)
"Delete the first sentence. Did the message improve? Usually yes. Start with the fact."
Hour 4: Finance & Operations
60 min

Goal: Use the synthetic data pack. Prove AI can reconcile ledgers, spot maintenance patterns, and write SOPs.

Data Safety Check: Ensure everyone is using the synthetic CSV/JSON files. Stop anyone trying to paste real SAP exports.
Watch For
People accepting AI math blindly. Remind them: AI flags, you verify.
Debrief Question
"If you ran this ledger check every week, how much time would it save?"
Rescue Line (Vague SOPs)
"Your 'Done When' step is vague. Tell AI to write it as a condition a new operator can objectively check."
Hour 5: Sales & HR
60 min

Goal: Personalisation at scale. Pitching the same profile to two different buyers, and drafting accurate JDs.

Watch For
JDs that invent fake certifications. HR must catch this.
Debrief Question
"Which words in the Architect pitch would never appear in the Builder pitch?"
Rescue Line (Invented facts)
"Ask AI: which of these skills did you infer vs which did I provide? Replace inferred ones with [confirm]."
Hour 6: Prompt to Asset
60 min

Goal: Convert one-off prompts into reusable team templates with [BRACKETED] variables.

Watch For
Vague variables like [INFO]. Force them to be specific like [PO NUMBER].
Debrief Question
"Which of these templates would save the whole VEKA plant the most time?"
Rescue Line (Not a template)
"If I gave this to a new joiner on day one, would they know exactly what to type in the brackets?"
Hour 7: Capstone Sprint
60 min

Goal: Build and test a real solution to a real bottleneck. Test it 3 times.

Watch For
Vague projects ("Transform procurement"). Force them down to a specific task.
Gate Check
Do they have an input ready to test with? If no, they cannot build.
Rescue Line (Scope creep)
"If you cannot complete this sentence: 'Every week I spend 1 hour on X and the output is Y' — your project is too vague."
Hour 8: Showcase & Sustain
60 min

Goal: 90-second demos, final leaderboard scoring, and the written 30-day plan commitment.

Watch For
Demos that don't show the Before state. The Before is what makes the After impressive.
Action
Announce the winner, award the badge, close the day on a high.
Rescue Line (Weak 30-day plan)
"Don't write 'I will use AI more'. Write exactly what task you will do with it this coming Monday."
