🏷️The 3 Data Tiers

Every piece of VEKA data falls into one of three tiers. When you're not sure which, treat it as the more sensitive one.

🟢 Public

  • Published brochures & catalogues
  • Website content
  • General product specs already public

🟡 Internal

  • Internal SOPs and process notes
  • Generic training material
  • Internal memos with no client info

🔴 Confidential

  • Dealer pricing & discounts
  • Customer contracts & personal data
  • Technical drawings & unreleased designs

🔏Anonymising Before You Upload

✅ Do

  • Replace dealer/customer names with IDs
  • Strip phone, PAN, GST and address before it leaves your machine
  • Remove pricing tied to a specific named client
  • Check whether a generic, aggregated version works instead

❌ Don't

  • Upload a raw quotation with the client's name and price still on it
  • Assume "just a quick check" makes it safe
  • Paste an entire customer contract just to summarise it
  • Wait until later to think about this — start now

⚖️DPDP 2023 — What It Means for VEKA

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023) governs how personal data — names, phone numbers, addresses, ID numbers — can be collected, used and stored. For VEKA, this mostly touches dealer and customer contact details.

What counts as "personal data"

  • Anything that identifies a person: name, phone, email, address, PAN
  • Still personal data even without a name, if it can be traced back to someone

What it means day to day

  • Have a clear purpose before using someone's personal data in a new tool
  • A public AI tool is a third party — sending it personal data can count as a data transfer
  • When in doubt: anonymise first, ask compliance/IT second
📋The Draft VEKA AI Usage Policy
  1. Only use company-approved AI tools for confidential work
  2. Never paste customer personal data, pricing, or contracts into a public/free AI tool
  3. Verify any technical spec or standard AI gives you before using it
  4. When unsure if data is Internal or Confidential, treat it as Confidential
  5. Report any accidental data upload to IT/compliance immediately
  6. AI output is a draft — a human always reviews before it goes external
