🔑 Data Classification — 3 Tiers

Before you type anything into any AI tool, ask: which tier does this data belong to?

🟢 Public

Already published or safe to share with anyone — brochures, website copy, public price lists, press releases.

🔵 Internal

Not secret, but not for outsiders — SOPs, org charts, internal schedules, non-sensitive meeting notes.

🔴 Confidential

Would cause real harm if leaked — formulations, dealer margins, personal data, unpublished financials, vendor bank details.

Part 4⏱ 15 min

🌍 India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — In Practical Terms

The DPDP Act 2023 is India's data privacy law. VEKA holds personal data of customers, dealers, and employees — so it applies to us. Here's what actually matters day to day:

  • VEKA is a "Data Fiduciary" — the organisation that decides why and how personal data is processed. We're accountable for it.
  • The customer/dealer/employee is the "Data Principal" — the person the data is about. They have rights over it.
  • Consent matters — personal data should generally only be collected and used for a clear, notified purpose.
  • Data Principals can request access, correction, or erasure of their personal data — and VEKA must be able to respond.
  • Data breaches must be reported — to the Data Protection Board of India and affected individuals, without unreasonable delay.
  • Penalties are serious — up to ₹250 crore for failing to take reasonable security safeguards.
  • Practical rule of thumb: anonymised or aggregated data (no way to identify a person) is much lower risk than raw personal data — which is exactly why Part 5 teaches you to anonymise before you prompt.

Part 5⏱ 20 min

🔒 Anonymising Dealer Data, Quotations & Reports

Anonymisation means removing or masking anything that could identify a person or reveal confidential business terms, before you paste text into an AI tool. If you must use AI on real dealer/customer data, strip it first.

  • Replace names with roles: "Mr. Rajesh Kumar" → "[the customer]"
  • Remove phone numbers, addresses, ID numbers (Aadhaar/PAN) entirely
  • Remove exact prices, margins, and discount percentages — describe them qualitatively if needed, or add them back yourself after
  • Remove internal codes for dies, formulations, and unpublished drawings

Part 6⏱ 20 min

🔍 Detecting AI Hallucinations in Technical Specs, Standards & Calculations

AI models predict plausible-sounding text — they don't "look up" facts unless specifically designed to. This means an AI tool can state a fake standard clause, an invented number, or a wrong calculation with total confidence. For a manufacturer quoting wind-loads, U-values, and BIS/EN standards, this is one of the most dangerous risks.

  • Never quote an AI-generated standard clause number, technical value, or calculation to a customer or architect without independent verification.
  • Cross-check standard numbers against the official BIS/EN source document.
  • Have a qualified engineer re-derive any safety-critical calculation (wind load, U-value, structural rating).
  • Be extra suspicious when an AI answer sounds unusually precise and confident — hallucinations often "feel" the most authoritative.
