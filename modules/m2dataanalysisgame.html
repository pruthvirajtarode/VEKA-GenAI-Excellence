🎮Game 1 · Spot the Red Flag+15 XP each

Review these data prompts and identify the critical error that will lead to a bad output.

Round 1 of 3
"Here is our sales data for Q1 and Q2. Why did sales drop in April?"
DiagnosisAI cannot know *why* sales dropped just by looking at a spreadsheet. It will hallucinate a reason. Fix: "What regions or product lines drove the drop in April?"
Round 2 of 3
"Here are 10,000 support tickets. Tell me the average time to resolution, the sentiment score, cluster them into 5 themes, and write an email to the team."
DiagnosisYou've asked it to perform heavy data crunching and creative writing in the same breath. Break it down step-by-step.
Round 3 of 3
"Analyze this list of 500 employee salaries and give me the average."
DiagnosisThe CEO's salary will skew the average. You should ask for the median to get a true picture of the typical employee.
📊The VEKA Dataset Quiz+15 XP each

Apply your skills to the synthetic VEKA dealer dataset. Can you spot the business issues?

Question 1
When Dealer X's total volume remains flat, but revenue drops significantly... what is happening?
Correct!Mix erosion occurs when a dealer stops buying premium profiles and shifts entirely to budget lines, eroding margins while volume looks fine.
Question 2
If a region shows a spike in "Transit Damage" and "Delayed Loading" notes, what AI analysis should you run?
Correct!You want to isolate the variable. Are all these damages tied to a specific third-party logistics provider?
