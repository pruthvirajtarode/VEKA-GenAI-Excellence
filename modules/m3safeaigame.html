Before you copy-paste anything into an AI model, you must know what tier it falls under.

🎮Game 1 · Is This Safe?+15 XP each

Look at the scenario and determine if it's safe to paste into a public AI tool (like free ChatGPT).

Round 1 of 4
Pasting a list of 50 customer emails and phone numbers to ask the AI to format them as a table.
Correct!PII is strictly confidential under the DPDP Act. Never upload it to public AI.
Round 2 of 4
Uploading VEKA's new, unreleased 2026 window profile CAD drawings to ask for marketing copy.
Correct!Unreleased IP is strictly confidential. Public AI tools may train on your uploads, leaking your IP to competitors.
Round 3 of 4
Copy-pasting the text from VEKA's public 'About Us' page to generate social media posts.
Correct!If it's already on the public website, it's Tier 1 (Public) and perfectly safe to use anywhere.
Round 4 of 4
You want to rewrite an employee's performance review. You replace their name with "Employee X" but leave their exact sales numbers, region, and direct manager's name.
Correct!Removing just the name isn't enough if the surrounding context makes it obvious who you are talking about. This violates the VEKA rule of true anonymization.
